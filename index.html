FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it safe with anti-cheat games?

SideDim never touches the game. It doesn't inject code, hook the keyboard or read game memory. It only places normal see-through windows on your other monitors and uses the standard Windows shortcut system. That's the same kind of thing many overlay and tray apps do.

Does it work with Discord, WhatsApp, Teams…?

Yes. SideDim listens to normal Windows notifications, so any app that shows them works: Discord, WhatsApp, Telegram, Teams, Slack, Outlook and more. If an app's notifications are turned off in Windows, SideDim can't see them either. The app's Recent notifications list shows exactly what arrived.

Does the dimming show up when I stream or record?

No, not by default. SideDim hides its layers from screen capture, so OBS, Game Bar and Discord screen share record your screens normally. You can turn this off if you want the dimming in your video.

Will it slow down my game?

It's built to be tiny. Dimming is a still, see-through layer the GPU draws for free. The effects use a few percent of one CPU core while they play, then rest. It never runs on the monitor with your game.

Does it actually lower my monitor's brightness?

By default it darkens with a layer, which works on every monitor. If your monitor supports DDC/CI you can also let SideDim turn down the real backlight. It's always put back, even after a crash.

Do I need an account? What data do you collect?

No account. We don't collect anything: no analytics, no telemetry. Settings are stored in a small file on your PC.

Which Windows versions?

Windows 11, and Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer). You need two or more monitors to get the most out of it.
